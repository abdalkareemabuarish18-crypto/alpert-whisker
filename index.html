HOW DOES IT WORK?

From enemy detection to autonomous attack.

01. ENEMY DETECTION

The robot uses infrared and opponent-detection sensors to continuously scan its surroundings. When another robot enters the detection range, the sensors send signals to the Arduino controller.

02. INTELLIGENT PROCESSING

The Arduino receives the sensor readings and determines the opponent's direction. It processes the information and decides whether the robot should move forward, turn left, or turn right.

03. HIGH-SPEED MOVEMENT

When an opponent is detected, the controller activates the motor driver. The motors move the robot forward at high speed to push the opponent out of the sumo arena.

04. FOUR ROTATING VISUAL ELEMENTS

Our AW-001 concept features four animated rotating energy elements surrounding its body. They represent the robot's aggressive futuristic design. The animation is decorative: actual competition robots must use permitted, guarded, non-dangerous mechanisms and follow the tournament's safety regulations.

05. EDGE DETECTION & SAFETY

Line sensors monitor the arena floor. If the robot approaches the white boundary, the controller gives edge avoidance priority. It reverses or changes direction to stay inside the arena.
